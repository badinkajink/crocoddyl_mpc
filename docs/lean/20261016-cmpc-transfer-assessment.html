Terms and metrics

MPC is model predictive control; RL is reinforcement learning. Displacement is maximum sole-origin distance from the interval's first sample, in mm. The comparison table covers 4.50–6.02 s. Contact loss counts 2 ms samples without a floor witness above 1 N; loaded slip is tangential material-point speed above 5 N contact load. Residual is root-mean-square (RMS) error between the 20 ms executable-torque prediction and next estimated sole position. Reach is hand-to-target distance. Tracking is commanded-minus-physical joint-position RMS in mrad. Runtime p95 is the 95th percentile over 302 evaluations, including baseline audit or correction filtering.

Force cone excess measures violation of a model linear inequality: force rows use N and moment rows N m, with a 1e-6 acceptance tolerance. Forces are world-aligned at each contact origin. Normal force is positive in compression. Centre-of-pressure margin is distance inside the predictor's 200 × 80 mm rectangle; the physical collision sole is 250 mm long and up to 76 mm wide. CAD means computer-aided design geometry. Recovery requires 0.3 s of loaded feet, table load below 5 N, joint/wrist speed below 0.1 rad/s and 0.03 m/s, standing joint RMS below 0.1 rad, base height within 50 mm, sole displacement below 5 mm and sole speed below 0.02 m/s. These thresholds are simulation assumptions.

On 100 nominal and 75 reserved pulse transitions, known servo evolution improves angular/joint-velocity residual over 2 ms frozen torque by 4.87%/12.18%, failing the requirement that both improve by at least 10%. Previous commands violate model cones at 75/100 and 57/75 observed endpoints.

No physical-parameter fitting, online model update, scenario MPC or adapted closed-loop comparison was performed. Hardware uncertainty ranges remain unidentified.

Future work

E1: Contact-aware gripper/wrist unloading with a planned clearance path, compared with scheduled release on the same nominal/pulse tasks and 9 s deadline. Reject if nominal table load remains above 5 N, disturbed recovery misses the original criteria, or motion/support worsens. Simulation thresholds require hardware calibration. Required data: H1, H2.

E2: Constrain known servo evolution over each command interval, retaining initial torque filtering as baseline; score every physical substep and next estimated endpoint. Reject if interval force violations or contact loss persist, reach worsens beyond the existing 5 mm allowance, or full controller latency exceeds 20 ms. Required data: H1, H2.

E3: Re-solve from a measured supported handover after foot load and velocity settle; compare the original approach and velocity correction at identical initialization. Persistent acceleration near 224 rad/s² rejects contact acquisition as a sufficient explanation; preserve reach and sole motion. Required data: H1, H3.

E4: After contact and command prerequisites pass, fit one identifiable physical parameter or select two or three models using earlier transitions; reserve a separate measured condition. Reject without lower reserved prediction error and task improvement at the same observation path, or if full latency exceeds 20 ms. No arbitrary uncertainty grid. Required data: H1, H2, H3.
