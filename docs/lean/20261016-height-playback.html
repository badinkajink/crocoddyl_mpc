Terms and metrics

Playback speed is simulation seconds per video second. Joint speed is the largest absolute logged velocity among the 27 actuated joints. Joint acceleration is the largest absolute change in joint velocity divided by the 10 ms logging interval. Bracing-wrist speed is the world-frame translational speed of the left wrist-yaw body origin. Reaching-hand speed uses the task point 130 mm along the right wrist-yaw body's local x axis. Point speeds use the replayed position Jacobian and logged generalized velocity. All peaks cover the full episode.

Playback verification and data

height_video.py previously selected every tenth row of a 100 Hz log and encoded at 30 fps. It now defaults to 1× and chooses the nearest logged state for each output timestamp. A constant three-row stride would instead produce 0.9× playback. All five new files contain 751 frames at 30 fps and last 25.033 s. The largest source-time rounding error is 3.34 ms; poses are not interpolated.

The timestamp checks cover 1×, 3×, 0.5×, irregular timestamps and invalid inputs. FFprobe independently confirms every output's duration and frame count. The h0885 images at 0, 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2.4 and 4 s were inspected, as were the other brace poses at 2.4 s and the standing fall at 6.5 s. For h0885, point velocities agree with position finite differences to 1.10 mm/s RMS per component at the reaching hand and 0.85 mm/s at the bracing wrist.

The measurements use archived CSVs under studies/runs/2026-09-08_height_dyn/ and the staged Lean_H12_Magpie.xml, with each table moved to its recorded height. CSV hashes, video hashes and timing are in the adjacent JSON files. The 100 Hz logs can miss faster peaks. Reconstructed point kinematics use the current staged model; no new dynamics or hardware trials were run.

Future work

The queued foot-motion experiment now includes startup speed and acceleration, plus videos at labelled 1×. A subsequent timing comparison should re-solve with 120 versus 240 approach nodes at 20 ms, retaining the 35-node MPC horizon. Measure startup acceleration, wrist speed at contact and foot displacement alongside reach error. A slower approach that retains the startup spike would point to initialization or command tracking as the next cause to test. Hardware velocity and torque tracking logs are needed to choose executable limits.
