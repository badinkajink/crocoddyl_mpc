Terms and metrics

Post-pulse measures 4.50–6.02 s; hold begins at 4.00 s. Displacement is maximum sole-centre distance from the interval’s first sample, in mm. Contact loss is time without a floor witness above 1 N, sampled every 2 ms. Loaded slip is horizontal material-point velocity at floor contacts above 5 N, in m/s. Residual is RMS distance from the next estimated sole position to a 20 ms prediction re-evaluated using the initial executable servo torque. Solver residual instead uses its shooting trajectory. State residual separates translation (mm), orientation/joint displacement (rad), base linear velocity (m/s) and angular/joint velocity (rad/s).

Normal force is signed world-z compression at the contact origin, in N; negative means tensile. Tangent is horizontal force magnitude. Cone excess is positive distance outside a linear bound: force rows use N, moment rows N m. Centre-of-pressure (CoP) margin is minimum distance inside the model’s world-aligned 200 × 80 mm sole rectangle, evaluated above 5 N. The simulated mesh margin uses normal-weighted witnesses and the moving collision sole polygon. A negative model-rectangle margin can coexist with valid mesh support. Tracking is joint-command RMS error in rad. Runtime is measured on one CPU; p95 is the 95th percentile.

Measured failure and intervention

The preceding foot-motion checkpoint measured tensile nominal forearm force before the 90 N pulse and severe disturbed wrench violations. The state-frame checkpoint resolved the tested coordinate change. Reactive reanchoring already changed state and force by exactly zero with foot gains [0, 50]; that experiment is closed. This checkpoint therefore tests the requested force-feasibility prerequisite.

From 4.00 s, a 27-variable quadratic program minimizes squared change in initial total servo torque. Analytic contact-force derivatives provide an affine force map at the estimated state. Existing wrench/friction-cone rows, actuator-force bounds, position-input bounds and the runtime’s 90% feedforward clamp are enforced. The optimizer is the installed SciPy SLSQP implementation; no dependency was installed. Position and velocity commands remain those of MPC; feedforward is adjusted to produce the projected total torque. Acceptance requires at most 0.000001 N or N m row excess. If no feasible torque exists, the implemented fallback uses estimated joint position, zero desired velocity and zero feedforward; no final trial invoked it.

Both baseline and correction use the archived h0885 elbow+forearm plan, 35 nodes at 0.02 s, one BoxFDDP iteration and default_sense(seed=17). Its 10 mm/0.01 rad base-error assumptions and ideal joint/velocity channels lack hardware calibration. Ground truth is used for scoring and rendering only. The 90 N world +y reaching-wrist pulse remains at 4.50–4.80 s. Friction, foot geometry, contact memberships and certified defaults are unchanged. The hypothesis required feasible issued hold commands, at least 10% lower disturbed left-sole residual, no added nominal contact loss and at most 5 mm added nominal final reach error.
