Transfer assessment and Future work

Simulation rejects low-motion dwell as a sufficient general release correction. The disturbed improvement accompanies a 2 s delay, and nominal contact unloading regresses. The remaining model-based question is whether planned gripper/wrist clearance and measured support state can select a feasible unloading path. A later comparison should retain this baseline and reject a proposed path if its nominal table load remains above 5 N or disturbed recovery still misses the fixed deadline.

Hardware validation needs synchronized table-relative gripper/wrist clearance and normal load through unloading, issued commands, encoder/current response, estimated base/joint state and independent sole motion. Loaded contact geometry, friction/compliance and transport delay require measurement before choosing hardware thresholds. The present sensing path uses synthetic pose bias and ideal velocity channels; no hardware trial or new queue was started. The separate transfer-review checkpoint remains unperformed here.
