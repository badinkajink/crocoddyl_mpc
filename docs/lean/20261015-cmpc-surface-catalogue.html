Terms and metrics: A body frame is the MuJoCo link coordinate system, before its inertial-frame transform. Local points are in metres. Normals are unit vectors directed out of the robot; compressive support force points in the opposite direction. An allowed patch is a connected union of coplanar CAD triangles, including its holes, with area in mm². Site displacement is the Euclidean distance from the old site to the candidate point in the same body frame, in mm. Height is world point height minus the tabletop plane, in mm; a negative value indicates intersection. A proxy is a simplified collision shape. Signed proxy separation is negative inside that shape. Vertex error is the maximum nearest-vertex distance in both directions after transforming corresponding meshes to the same body frame. RSS is peak resident memory.

Boundary-distance bound is an upper bound on distance from every point in the allowed region to its collision boundary, in mm; zero means coincident surfaces. Disk diameter is twice 90% of the seed triangle’s inradius, in mm, giving a circular footprint contained in the region. A collision record is a MuJoCo contact candidate after forward evaluation; it can include a positive gap within the collision margin. A robot-side witness is the surface position reconstructed from that record’s midpoint, signed gap and normal. Acceptance requires the specified body and geom, projected triangle-union membership, at most 0.5 mm plane distance and at most 25° normal difference. It measures geometric membership, without estimating force.
