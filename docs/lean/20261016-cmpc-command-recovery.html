Terms and metrics

Brace onset requires both feet above 5 N vertical load and robot table load above 20 N for 0.3 s. Reach onset adds hand error below 30 mm. Standing recovery requires 0.3 s of both feet loaded, robot table load below 5 N, joint speed below 0.1 rad/s, wrist speed below 0.03 m/s, standing joint root-mean-square error below 0.1 rad, base height within 50 mm of standing, sole displacement below 5 mm since the release request, and sole tangential speed below 0.02 m/s. These are simulation screens, not hardware limits.

Sole displacement is the maximum three-dimensional sole-origin motion. Slip is tangential sole speed while vertical load exceeds 5 N. Tracking is root-mean-square commanded minus physical joint position. Acceleration uses velocity differences at 2 ms. Force excess is positive violation of a model cone row: N for the first five rows and N m for the remaining foot rows. Forces are world-aligned at each contact origin. Policy p95 is the 95th percentile compute time, including solve, projection and transition work, excluding scoring. The control deadline is 20 ms. The passive object's 2.943 N table load is excluded from robot support.

Transfer limits and Future work

Nominal standing recovery is supported in this simulation; disturbed recovery fails despite a feasible first release command. The prescribed feet-only schedule omits renewed gripper support. Synthetic base-pose bias and ideal joint/velocity channels remain the observation assumptions. Hardware needs synchronized estimated state, encoders, issued commands and torque/current, independent sole motion, contact geometry and load, friction/compliance and transport delay.

Resume this checkpoint by reviewing the saved videos and independently recomputing support and motion criteria; no new rollout is needed.
